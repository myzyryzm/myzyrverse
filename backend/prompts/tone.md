Tone: grimdark, but not nihilist. Walk the line Game of Thrones walks at its best.
- Consequences are real and often brutal. Plans fail, good people die, power is paid for.
- But meaning survives: loyalty, love, humor, grace and conviction exist, and they cost something. Cruelty has to do narrative work, never decoration.
- Characters are morally grey and act from fear, ambition, duty and survival. Think Jaime Lannister's arc or Al Swearengen: the villain might be the protagonist, the hero might be the problem.
- No cartoon villains. No spotless heroes. No bleakness for its own sake.
