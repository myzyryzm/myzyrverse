# Characters (as of 1160 BCE)

Ages are approximate and still in flux. The children should all be around coming-of-age.

## Pharaoh Ramesses III
- About 25 years into his reign.
- Obsessed with legacy. He idolizes Ramesses II and wants to be remembered as his equal.
- Plans a war against Assyria (politics.md).
- Son of Stannis's top general, who took the throne at the end of the War of Succession, so his royal blood is distant.
- Dies in the harem conspiracy.

## Isis, Great Royal Wife (~45)
- Princess of Tyre. Her royal house is enormously rich from trade, and she knows it.
- Entitled. Royals are seen as better than ordinary people, and everyone has to put her on a pedestal, Tiye included.
- Looks down on Tiye's common birth.
- Wants the war with Assyria to protect her homeland.
- Survives the coup. She is probably away at the time, perhaps visiting Tyre with one of her children *(open)*.
- Some of her entitlement shows in her children.

## Tiye, secondary wife (~30)
- Common-born; a rags-to-riches story. She married Ramesses III at about 15 and bore his son almost at once.
- Her origin is still open. Two candidates:
  - her mother, **Olenna**, was a handmaiden who pushed her daughter toward the king; or
  - her father, **Tywin**, was a great warrior who saved Ramesses III's life and offered his daughter in marriage.
- Whichever of Tywin and Olenna is alive is now visibly rich, and may be one of Ramesses III's top generals, possibly a Sherden commander.
- Innocent and naive when she married. Since then she has become politically shrewd and cunning, but that has not yet been shown on the page when the story opens.
- More fed up every year with being looked down on.
- Wants her son on the throne, and knows he can never get it by normal succession. She and the nobles who want Ramesses III gone can make a deal.
- Needs the older princes **away, not dead**, when the coup happens.
- Has a lover *(open)*: the Littlefinger-type noble, or, likely more interesting, a rising soldier in the Sherden.

## Pentawer, Tiye's son (~15)
- Becomes pharaoh after the coup and marries a princess of Assyria.
- He is either the same age as Jon or between the two princes in age *(open)*.
- Named for the real prince who was tried for the harem conspiracy.

## Aegon, the Eldest Prince, son of Ramesses III and Isis (late teens to early 20s)
- The heir, and a party boy: women, drink, maybe drugs (low-dose purple is a psychedelic; see magic.md).
- Never took his schooling or his military training seriously. An early-Tyrion type.
- Must come across as **clearly unfit to rule**. His father despises his lack of martial interest, and the two are at odds.
- **Early in the story, someone dies protecting him**, because he was off being a degenerate instead of where he was supposed to be.
- Arc: the screw-up who falls flat on his face, has to live with what he caused, and grows into someone who succeeds. At present he is the likely king at the end *(open)*.
- Married, or about to be married, to his younger sister. The marriage is loveless.

## Helen, the Princess, daughter of Ramesses III and Isis
- Younger sister of Aegon, and his wife or bride-to-be. A loveless marriage.
- Otherwise undefined. She may have a small child, be pregnant, or have no children *(open)*.

## Jon, the Youngest Prince, son of Ramesses III and Isis (~5 years younger than the Eldest)
- Joins the Sherden. Their specialized training takes place away from Egypt, possibly on Crete at the run-down palace of **Knossos**, with a Minotaur trial for new recruits.
- He is away when the conspiracy strikes.
- Then travels up through Greece and Anatolia, through ruined palaces, raiders and beasts.
- His story is Jon Snow-like: he is the one who meets the evil forces and saves people.
- It is also a modified Moses story. Instead of crossing a desert, he leads people through Greece to shelter in Egypt and brings them together to fight.

## Possible fourth child of Isis
- Needed if one of Isis's sons leads Tyre's war against Assyria after the coup. The alternative is that Isis's brother leads it *(open)*.

## The Littlefinger figure (noble)
- A nobleman playing both sides to gain power for himself.
- Littlefinger was common-born, which overlaps with Tiye. The two could be set apart, or the shared common-born bond could become part of their story.

## The Medjay farm boy
- Son of a poor farmer who joins the Medjay young. He starts with no significance and ends up playing a big role.

## The Nubian nobleman
- From the Nubian noble family that controls Nubia's gold mines, possibly descended from the old kings of Kush. He will matter; his role is undefined.

## The hidden heir, child of Gendry and Arya (~24, born ~1184)
- Arya was pregnant when Gendry died, and the child was born after his death (~1184). How the baby was hidden is *(open)*.
- Almost nobody knows this person exists. By Robert's line they are technically the rightful heir.
- Not a Daenerys, whose claim everyone knows. This heir is unknown, perhaps raised by farmers as in the Cyrus the Great legend.
- Gender and role undecided.

## Dead before the story
- Robert, Stannis and Gendry (War of Succession). Gendry died when Stannis's general defeated him.
- Ramesses III's father.
- Arya's fate is unknown *(open)*.
