# Factions

## The Sherden
- The elite of the elite: King's Guard, Praetorian Guard and Secret Service in one.
- They fight with broadswords rather than the sickle-sword (khopesh) *(weapon to confirm)*.
- Their specialized training costs money, so it is open only to those who can pay. Royals do their military service with them.
- **Relatively new.** Ramesses II founded them by recruiting Sea Peoples.
  - The first Sherden were rewarded with good land, somewhere in southern Canaan or Egypt *(open)*.
  - Their descendants are well-off rather than super rich, and service passes from father to son.
- **Two kinds of recruit:** sons of Sherden families, and sons of nobles and royals who can pay for the training.
- They look down on the Medjay.
- Their training may take place abroad, possibly on Crete.
- **Possible dark secret** *(undecided)*: a super-soldier program.
  - They abduct Greek and Sea Peoples warriors, or take them in battle, and inject them with purple.
  - Most die or go mad. The survivors become monsters, like the Mountain after his transformation.

## The Medjay
- An old institution, founded at the start of the New Kingdom to drive out the Hyksos. They began as elite soldiers.
- Now they are effectively the government service, and respect depends on the branch:
  - elite warriors, mostly scouts and light-armored fighters (respected)
  - police
  - border patrol (respected)
  - tomb guards (respected)
  - tax collectors (despised)
- Most boys grow up to be farmers. For a poor farmer's son, rising in the Medjay is the way to make money.
- There is tension with the Sherden, who look down on them.
