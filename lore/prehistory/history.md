# History before the story

## Ramesses II "the Great" (r. 1275–1210, born ~1300)
- Brutal, and very successful. His reign is the high-water mark of the New Kingdom.
- He founded the Sherden by recruiting Sea Peoples (factions.md).
- At the end of his reign, trade still ran from Scandinavia and Britain to Iberia and all around the Mediterranean.

## His son (r. 1210–~1190)
- Ruled about 20 years and was fairly successful. His name is not yet set; the historical parallel is Merneptah.
- His death started the War of Succession.

## The War of Succession (1190–1185)
- Two grandsons of Ramesses II fought over the throne: the elder, **Robert**, and the younger, **Stannis**.
- Robert died. His son **Gendry** took up the claim as rival pharaoh and killed his uncle Stannis.
- Gendry's wife was **Arya**, a Hittite princess. The marriage was the Hittites' last entanglement in Egyptian affairs before their own fall.
- **Stannis's top general** then defeated Gendry and avenged his master. **Gendry died** in that defeat.
  - He was not a direct descendant of Ramesses II, but had some royal blood: "a second son of a second son of a third daughter."
  - He took the throne around 1185 and died in battle while taking it, or after a very short reign.
  - His son is **Ramesses III**. The historical parallel is Setnakhte, founder of the 20th Dynasty.
- The war badly weakened the New Kingdom.
- Arya was already pregnant when Gendry died. Their child was born in secret, and nobody knows about it (characters.md, "The hidden heir").

## Ramesses III's reign so far (~1185–1160)
- He has brought back some of Egypt's glory and strength, but it is nowhere near what it was under Ramesses II.
- He fought off **Libyan** invasions in the west and **Sea Peoples** invasions from the north, and won. He has not expanded Egypt's reach.
- The wars were very expensive. The nobility resents the cost but cannot say so.
- He married a princess of Tyre, Isis, to draw northern Canaan back into Egypt's orbit.

## The wider Collapse
- Over the last ~50 years, long-distance trade has shrunk badly because of the Drought and Sea Peoples raids.
- The Hittite empire has fallen, and so has its capital, **Hattusa**.
- Most Mycenaean palaces are destroyed. Greece is now a land of raiders and warlords.
- Over the last few decades **Tyre** has risen to dominate northern Canaan, which used to sit in Egypt's sphere.

## Intentional divergences from the historical record
- The harem conspiracy succeeds. Historically it failed and the conspirators were tried (the Judicial Papyrus of Turin).
- Ramesses II reigns 1275–1210 here (historically ~1279–1213). His son reigns ~20 years (Merneptah ruled ~10).
- The succession war here is two brothers plus a nephew. The real late 19th Dynasty had Seti II vs. Amenmesse, then Siptah and Twosret.
- The Great Royal Wife is a princess of Tyre. Historically she was Iset Ta-Hemdjert, origin unknown.
- Assyria is expanding west and raiding northern Canaan.
- Tiye's son keeps his historical name, **Pentawer**. In history he was tried for the conspiracy; here he wins the throne.
