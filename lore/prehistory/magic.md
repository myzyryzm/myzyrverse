# Magic, the Drought, and creatures

## Principles
- Magic works mysteriously, as in GRRM: nothing systematized, no wizards casting spells, no fire from the hands, no Jedi.
- The magic comes from the substance that makes **Tyrian purple**. Separately, mythical creatures exist.

## The purple
- The substance that makes Tyrian purple dye, which comes in a few shades.
- **Low doses:** a psychedelic, taken recreationally.
- **High doses, over time:** powers. They are subtle, never spell-casting. Only one power is defined so far: people who have it can communicate with one another across distance. More powers will be added.
- **Worked into weapons and armor:** makes them stronger and sharper. Whether it works with bronze, with iron (rare in this era), or with both is undecided.
- **Massive injected doses:** cause physical transformation.
- When it was discovered is undecided.

## The purple guild *(name undecided)*
- Modeled on Dune's Spacing Guild, except that it controls the flow of **information** rather than travel.
- Takes children young and feeds them purple until they gain powers. Trained members work like **payphones**.
- In this world, information travels fast and people travel slowly, like the era after the telephone but before the car.
- **Its own entity.** It may be religious (tied to the Egyptian priesthood, for example) or independent. Its base may be Cyprus or Egypt.

## Werewolves
- Created by an experiment: **wolf's blood plus purple, injected into a human**.
- They live in mainland Greece, along with ordinary wolves.
- Mythic echo: King Lycaon of Arcadia, whom Zeus turned into a wolf.

## Super soldiers *(undecided)*
- The Sherden may inject captured Greek and Sea Peoples warriors with purple (see factions.md).
- A darker turn: most die or go mad, and the survivors become monsters.

## Cyclopes
- Real. The Mycenaean palaces, with their giant Cyclopean masonry, were originally Cyclops homes and neighborhoods.
- Men came, drove the Cyclopes out, and took the palaces, possibly thousands of years ago. Since then the Cyclopes have lived in caves.
- The Drought is now forcing them out of the caves, which causes unrest in Greece.

## The Drought
- The outside force of pure evil, this world's **White Walkers**. It stands for the climate change that really drove the Bronze Age Collapse.
- It is already shrinking trade and pushing the Cyclopes out.
- It brings beasts with it (working names: drought walkers, drought runners).
- **Undecided:**
  - what the beasts look like;
  - where they come from: Anatolia as an epicenter, from the north only, or also across the Sahara into Egypt.
