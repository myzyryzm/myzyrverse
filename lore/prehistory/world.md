# Myzyrverse: world overview

Historical fantasy set in the eastern Mediterranean around **1160 BCE**, during the Bronze Age Collapse. Chapters use rotating close-third POV, as in ASOIAF. Tone: grimdark but not nihilist.

## Premise
- The story opens shortly before the **harem conspiracy** against Pharaoh **Ramesses III**, about 25 years into his reign.
- **Alt-history:** the conspiracy **succeeds**. The secondary wife, **Tiye**, puts her son on the throne.
- Real dates are shifted a few years to fit character ages. Some real events are kept.
- **No royal children die in the coup.** None of the children of Ramesses III and the Great Royal Wife are killed while the throne is taken; they end up scattered, not slaughtered. The Great Royal Wife survives too.

## The fantasy layer (see magic.md)
- Magic works mysteriously, as in GRRM: no wizards, no spells, no fire from the hands.
- All of it comes from the substance that makes **Tyrian purple**.
- Mythical creatures are real: Cyclopes, werewolves, and the beasts that come with the Drought.
- **The Drought** is this world's outside force of pure evil, its White Walkers. It is the real climate change of the Bronze Age Collapse made into an antagonist.
