griffin are flightless. but can jump really far (like other big cats can). their specialized biology (light head b/c bird but strong as hell b/c big cat) gives em that unique ability. they are not ridden animals and grow to about the size of a cave lion. this one will be juvenile. ancient griffins were used to pull chariots and in general fighting. never ridden like a horse. griffins are natural animals. griffin is not this magic animal like a dragon. it's more so a creature that is thought to have died out (or at least become much rarer).
