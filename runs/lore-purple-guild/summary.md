This lore run settles the purple guild's backstory and present form. The guild is a religious college based on Cyprus, independent of every kingdom. It descends from a Minoan palace priesthood that discovered purple's power around 1800 BCE and fled by sea when Knossos fell. Its power comes from the unsunned "draw", the gland secretion before light touches it. Tyre controls the murex beds, and its rise rests on them. The guild does all processing itself, in dark vault-workshops, and none of it on the mainland. Sunlight changes the potency. Weapon-working uses the raw precursor, and the sunned dregs feed a black-market recreational trade, which is what Aegon uses and what scars him. Nodes are always adults: they enter in youth, spend 10–14 years on a ladder ending in a lethal final dose, and are celibate, nameless and bound for life. They link only to other nodes and relay word for word under an oath that is truly kept and enforced by death. The guild serves whoever holds Egypt's throne. It carries the conspirators' traffic and Ramesses III's own in the same season without warning him. A Senior Node, a new POV character, and a Pi-Ramesses node are established.
